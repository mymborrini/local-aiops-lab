# Observability concept

## SLI

SLI (Service Level Indicator) is a quantitative measurement of a service's reliability or performance, such as availability, latency, or error rate.

## SLO

SLO (Service Level Objective) is a target that defines the desired level of reliability or performance for a service.

## SLA

SLA (Service Level Agreement) is a formal agreement with customers that defines the expected service level and may include consequences if it is not met.

## Error Budget

Error Budget is the amount of unreliability a service can tolerate while still meeting its SLO. It helps teams balance feature development and reliability work.

## MTTD - Mean Time To Detect

MTTD (Mean Time To Detect) is the average time it takes to detect a service incident after it occurs.

## MTTR - Mean Time To Recovery

MTTR (Mean Time To Recovery) is the average time required to restore a service after an incident has been detected.

## Toil 

Toil is repetitive, manual, operational work that provides little lasting value and could be reduced or eliminated through automation.
